Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

This policy covers the mobile games published by Oisif Games, including Hole City. It describes what the games collect, which third-party services receive it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

We do not ask you to create an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or postal address inside a game.

Advertising and your choices

Our games are free and funded by advertising. Ads may be personalised using your device advertising identifier.

  • On iOS, the game asks for permission before any tracking identifier is used. If you decline, or if you have turned off Allow Apps to Request to Track in your device settings, ads are shown without the advertising identifier.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, a consent prompt appears on first launch. Ads and analytics that rely on consent run only if you give it, and you can change your answer at any time from the game's settings screen.

Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent — personalised advertising, analytics where consent is required, and push notifications.
  • Performance of a contract — running the game, saving your progress and operating leaderboards.
  • Legitimate interests — fixing crashes, preventing fraud and abuse, and non-personalised advertising that keeps the game free.

How long we keep it

Gameplay and analytics data is retained for up to 24 months. Crash reports are retained for up to 12 months. Saved progress is kept while the player account stays active and for 24 months after the last session, then deleted. Our third-party providers apply their own retention periods, described in their policies above.

Where your data goes

The providers listed above process data on servers in the European Union and the United States. Transfers outside the EEA rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision, as set out in each provider's policy.

Children

Our games are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, write to support@oisifgames.com and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask us to give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or withdraw a consent you previously gave. Exercising these rights is free and we answer within 30 days.

Write to support@oisifgames.com. Because player accounts are anonymous, we may need details that let us locate your record — device model, country, and the rough dates you played. If we cannot identify you from what you send, we will say so rather than guess.

If you are in the EEA or the UK and you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to your national data protection authority.

Changes

When this policy changes we update the date at the top of this page. If a change materially affects how we handle your data, we will also say so in the game.
